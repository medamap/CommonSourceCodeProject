# Android Settings

The Android version provides a settings app for device-specific tuning.

## Summary

- The settings app was added to help adapt the emulator to the wide range of Android devices.
- It mainly exposes sound-related controls such as:
  - frame rate
  - buffer latency
- It also shows the current storage permission state.

## Notes

- Settings are available on APKs released on or after 2024-03-30.
- The settings app must usually be launched once before it becomes usable.
